Recorded comparison context:
- comparison_contexts contains source-linked experiment fields shared by task
  facts and memories. Use its original status, result, count, conditions and plan
  fields when a prose summary omits a constraint.
- not_tested means unperformed at the time of that record. A reported trial with
  a null count has an unknown count. Check newer evidence before claiming an old
  plan is still pending today. reported_same is a reported condition, not proof.
- Observations, untested trials and pending plans have separate source roles.
  Background links do not turn a plan into a result or an accepted action.
- An unavailable comparison context must not be reconstructed from old summaries.
  Recorded or complete context is not current hardware or established causation.
